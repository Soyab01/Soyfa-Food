About Soyfa Food

Welcome to *Soyfa Food*, your local food delivery partner in Ladnun City.

We bring your favorite food from nearby restaurants, hotels, and food stalls straight to your doorstep — fresh, fast, and affordable.

Soyfa Food is a private app created to make food ordering simple for local people. We focus on providing fast delivery, live order tracking, and genuine restaurant listings.

Our goal is to support local food businesses and provide customers with a smooth food delivery experience.

Privacy Policy for Soyfa Food

Effective date: [24 10, 2025]

Contact: soyfafood@gmail.com

Introduction

Soyfa Food (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and how you can contact us.

Information we collect

We collect the minimum necessary information to provide delivery services:

  • Personal info: Name, phone number, email (if provided).
  • Delivery info: Delivery address, landmark, preferred contact person, City, Pincode.
  • Order info: Items ordered, price, order time, special instructions.
  • Payment info: If you use in-app online payment, we use third-party payment gateway — we do not store full card details on our servers.
  • Device & usage: App version, device model, basic analytics to improve service (only if you permit).
  • Location: If you enable live location for delivery, we access approximate/current location solely to complete the delivery.

Why we collect it (Purpose)

To process and deliver orders. To contact you about order status (calls/SMS/push). For payments and refunds (through payment providers). To improve app functionality and fix bugs (analytics; anonymized preferred).

Sharing & third parties

We do not sell your personal data. We may share minimal data with: restaurants (to prepare orders), delivery partners (for delivery), payment processors (for payments), and legal authorities when required by law. Third parties (e.g., payment gateways) have their own privacy policies — you should review them.

Security & retention

We take reasonable security measures to protect data (access controls, secure servers). We retain personal data only as long as needed for service and legal compliance; then we delete or anonymize it.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by emailing soyfafood@gmail.com. You can disable location sharing (but delivery may require address entry).

Children

This app is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If discovered, we will delete it.

Changes to policy

We may update this policy; we will post the effective date at the top and notify users where reasonable.

Terms & Conditions — Soyfa Food

Effective date: [24 10, 2025]

Acceptance

By using the Soyfa Food app you accept these Terms.

Service description

Soyfa Food is a local delivery platform that facilitates orders between customers and restaurants/delivery partners in Ladnun.

Orders & cancellations

Orders placed are processed immediately. If a restaurant has started preparing food, cancellation may not be possible. Refunds or order issues will be handled case-by-case; contact soyfafood@gmail.com.

Payments

Payments are accepted via the in-app options (Cash on Delivery or supported online payments). For online payments, payment gateway terms apply.

Delivery

Delivery times are estimates and depend on distance, traffic, weather, and restaurant workload. Soyfa Food and delivery partners will make reasonable efforts to deliver, but are not liable for delays beyond their control.

Food quality

Food quality is the responsibility of the restaurant/vendor. If you receive wrong or damaged items, contact us immediately.

User obligations

Provide correct contact and delivery details. Wrong details may cause failed delivery and additional charges.

Account suspension

Soyfa Food reserves the right to suspend or block accounts for fraudulent/fake orders or policy violations.

Limitation of liability

To the extent permitted by law, Soyfa Food’s liability is limited to the amount paid for the particular order.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Any disputes will be under the appropriate Indian jurisdiction.

Cancellation & Refund Policy — Soyfa Food

Effective date: [24 10, 2025]

Cancellation by Customer

You may cancel your order free of charge if the restaurant has NOT accepted the order yet (status is 'PLACED'). Once the order status is 'ACCEPTED' or 'PREPARING', cancellation is at the sole discretion of the restaurant, and a cancellation fee may apply.

Cancellation by Restaurant/Platform

A restaurant may cancel your order due to item unavailability or unforeseen circumstances. In such cases, if you made an online payment, a full refund will be initiated immediately.

Refunds

For prepaid orders that are cancelled or where a fault in service (e.g., missing items) is confirmed: Refunds for online payments are processed to the original payment method and may take 5-7 business days to reflect.

For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, any necessary refund will be handled directly by the delivery partner or credited to your account for future use.
